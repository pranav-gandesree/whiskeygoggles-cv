About WhiskyGoggles

What is WhiskyGoggles?

WhiskyGoggles is an advanced whisky bottle identification application that uses computer vision and machine learning to identify bottles from images. Our goal is to help enthusiasts and collectors identify bottles and determine fair market prices.

How it Works

When you upload an image of a whisky bottle, our system:

  1. Extracts visual features using a deep learning model
  2. Recognizes text on the label using optical character recognition
  3. Compares the bottle against our extensive database
  4. Provides you with the most likely matches and pricing information

Why Use WhiskyGoggles?

WhiskyGoggles helps you:

  • Identify rare or unfamiliar bottles
  • Determine if you're paying a fair price
  • Learn about different whisky types and brands
  • Contribute to a community-driven pricing database
